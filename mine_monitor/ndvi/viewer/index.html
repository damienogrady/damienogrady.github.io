What you are seeing

Each year represents an April–July seasonal composite. Input years with fewer than five QA-valid scenes at a pixel are masked, not filled. The chart retains gaps. Landsat and Sentinel-2 are not cross-calibrated. A fitted line is a model of the signal, not a record of the individual observed values.

The area shown is the 10 km buffered 14-lease AOI (1,534,281 cells at 30 m), not a mine footprint. Classes are a rule-based adaptation, unvalidated. Same-year recovery candidates are not accepted as recovery. The 2022 disturbance-onset cohort is internal QA only.

Imagery attribution: Landsat — USGS/NASA; Sentinel-2 — Copernicus. Files are checked against the bundled manifest; those hashes do not authenticate the bundle itself.
