Site, data-quality and use disclosures

Analysis AOI: the 10,000 m buffer around the dissolved union of 14 leases (1,380.85 km²; 1,534,281 cells at 30 m). The lease union is approximately 196.72 km² (14.25% of the AOI). Areas are 30 m cell-area equivalents, not surveyed or statutory areas; buffered-AOI percentages are not percentages of the lease union.

Flood-prone HAND screening proxy only; not a statutory flood extent, flood hazard map, or 0.5% AEP floodplain.

This demonstration retains its 15 November 2026 tenure snapshot. A 19 November comparison found one 30 m cell outside the refreshed 10 km analysis area; that cell appears as a control in eight historical matching records, including four primary floor-2 records. Results have not been recalculated. Lease renewal status is not legally verified; do not use this snapshot to establish current tenure or for regulatory decisions.

Curragh thresholds are NOT general defaults.

Class outputs: rule-based adaptation, unvalidated, pending M4 supervised validation.

Trajectory-rate labels only: DHR/DLR fragility is 24.26% for Landsat and 7.19% for Sentinel-2; these are not rehabilitation-quality or completion-criteria verdicts.
