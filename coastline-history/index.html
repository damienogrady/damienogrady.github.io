A changing landscape

Watch the basins of Lake Carpentaria and Lake Bass appear and disappear as sea levels change. In the north-west, compare the shifting coastline of Borneo with the islands of Indonesia to its east.

These changing landscapes help give context to the Wallace Line, which runs between Borneo and Sulawesi and marks a boundary between the distinct faunal regions of Asia and Australasia.

At times of lower sea level, people could walk between what are now Papua New Guinea and mainland Australia. How the first people made the journey across the islands from Asia to Sahul remains a subject of research. I was fortunate to contribute to the study Early human settlement of Sahul was not an accident.

This map set grew out of work to create the background for And now for the weather … 130,000 years ago. In that video, land colours represent predicted vegetation; in this map, they represent height above the changing sea level.

BP means years before present. Sea level is relative to modern sea level; values between the 1,000-year observations are linearly interpolated. The coastline is shown using 142 video frames, so it is approximate between frames.
